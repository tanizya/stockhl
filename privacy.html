Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 28, 2026

Stock HiLo ("the App") is developed by an individual developer. This policy describes how the App handles your information.

Information We Collect

The App collects the minimum information necessary to provide its functionality:

  • Device Identifier — A per-app anonymous identifier (identifierForVendor) is used solely to prevent duplicate votes. This identifier is not shared with third parties and cannot be used to track you across other apps.
  • Vote Data — When you vote on a stock prediction (High/Low), your vote direction, the stock ticker, and the associated date are stored on our server to display community vote results.

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Photos, contacts, or calendar data
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA)
  • Financial or brokerage account information

Data Storage

Vote data is stored on Supabase (hosted on AWS). Your predictions are also saved locally on your device using standard app storage (UserDefaults) so they persist between sessions.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Supabase — For storing stock data and community votes. Supabase Privacy Policy

The App does not integrate any advertising SDKs, analytics frameworks, or social media trackers.

Data Retention

Vote data is retained on the server for the purpose of showing community results. There is no personally identifiable information associated with your votes.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please open an issue at github.com/tanizya/stockhlapp.
